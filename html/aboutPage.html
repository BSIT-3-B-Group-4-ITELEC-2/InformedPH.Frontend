OUR STORY

Who We Are

InformPH is an independent Philippine news platform committed to keeping Filipinos informed with clear, timely, and relevant reporting.

We cover the stories that shape everyday life—from national developments and business updates to lifestyle, community, and local news. Our goal is simple: make reliable information accessible and keep Filipino communities connected.

WHAT DRIVES US

Our Mission

To inform, connect, and empower Filipinos through stories that are accurate, useful, and grounded in the communities we serve.
